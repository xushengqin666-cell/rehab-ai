Rehab AI — Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026-08-23 · Last updated: 23 Aug 2026

1. 数据存储 · Data storage

本应用的全部数据（训练记录、评估、预约、计划、账号）默认只保存在你自己的设备浏览器（localStorage）中，不上传任何服务器。All data is stored locally in your device browser (localStorage) by default. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

2. 摄像头与视频 · Camera & video

摄像头画面仅用于在你设备本地运行 AI 姿态识别（MediaPipe，浏览器内运行）。视频画面绝不上传、不录制、不存储。The camera feed is used only for on-device AI pose analysis (MediaPipe, runs in your browser). Video is never uploaded, recorded or stored.

3. 账号与云同步 · Accounts & cloud sync

本地账号的密码以 PBKDF2 加密形式仅存本机。云同步为可选功能：只有当你主动配置自己的 Supabase 项目并登录后，数据才会加密传输并存到你自己的云端项目。我们（开发者）不运营、不接触任何用户数据服务器。Passwords are stored locally with PBKDF2 hashing. Cloud sync is optional: only after you configure your own Supabase project and log in is data transmitted to and stored in your own cloud project. The developer operates no user-data servers.

4. 二维码同步 · QR sync

二维码同步仅在你主动操作时，于两台设备之间直接传输数据，不经过任何服务器。QR sync transfers data directly between two devices only when you start it. No server is involved.

5. 数据删除 · Data deletion

随时可在 App「设置 → 清除全部数据」一键删除本机全部数据；云端数据请在你的 Supabase 项目或 App 中删除。Erase all local data anytime via Settings → Erase all data. Cloud data can be deleted from your Supabase project or the app.

6. 权限 · Permissions

  • 📷 摄像头（CAMERA）：仅用于实时姿态分析，可随时在系统设置中关闭。
  • 📷 Camera: used only for live pose analysis; revocable anytime in system settings.
  • 🔔 通知（可选）：仅用于你主动开启的训练提醒。
  • 🔔 Notifications (optional): only for reminders you enable.

7. 儿童与医疗 · Children & medical disclaimer

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。本应用仅供运动参考与科研用途，不构成医疗建议。This app is not directed at children under 13. It is for exercise reference and research only and does not provide medical advice.

8. 联系我们 · Contact

如有隐私问题请联系：xushengqin@qq.com 或通过 GitHub 仓库提 Issue。For privacy questions: xushengqin@qq.com or open an issue on the GitHub repository.
